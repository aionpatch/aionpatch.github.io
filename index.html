System, Hardware & UI

Market Protection, QoL & Engine Fixes

Market Gathering Restriction

Added a verification system that restricts the listing of gathering materials on the Market based on the account's gameplay history (anti-bot measure).

FSR Speed & 4K Monitor Crash Fix

Fixed longer loading times when using AMD FSR, and resolved crashes occurring at specific refresh rates on 4K monitors.

Dual GPU & Steam Cloud Config

Fixed graphics settings misconfiguration on PCs with two GPUs. Graphics settings are no longer saved to Steam Cloud to prevent cross-device overwrites.

Y-Axis Invert & Nameplate HP Bars

Added an option to invert the Y-axis in Settings, plus a toggle to show or hide HP bars under 'Show My Nameplate' settings.

Shugo Festival Reconnect Fix

Fixed an issue where player positions were reset when reconnecting to the network during the Shugo Festival.

Quick Answers & Featured Snippets

Frequently Asked Questions (Oct 14 Patch)

What changed with Odyle Energy (Bound) in the Oct 14 patch?

During the server transfer period, the weekly limit of 16 per server for Odyle Energy (Bound) in [Shop > Wind Breeze Merchants > Special] has been completely removed, and the weekly per-character purchase limit was increased from 4 to 20.

How many Halloween Pumpkin Coins can you earn in total?

You can earn 620 Coins from the 14-day Trick or Treat Attendance board (35/day, plus 100 on Days 7 and 14) and 235 Coins per week from the 10 Season Event Missions, totaling up to 1,325 Coins over the 3-week event.

What are the Sorcerer and Spiritmaster class fixes?

Sorcerer's Fire Wall, Cold Storm, and Bittercold Wind now properly apply bonus damage and Frost Cooldown Reduction on uneven elevation, and personal Embers/Frostbite DoTs are no longer overwritten by another player's stronger effect. Spiritmaster's Ancient Spirit (Lv. 15 or below) now activates skills properly.

How do you craft Candy Keys in AION 2?

Complete Expedition and Transcendence Dungeons to earn Candy Key Fragments. In Event Substance Morphs, combine 60 fragments to craft 1 Red, Yellow, or Blue Candy Key (once per week per server, 100% success) to open event candies in towns.

Quick Answers & Featured Snippets

Frequently Asked Questions (Ludra & Patch Hub)

What item level is required for the Ludra Raid in AION 2?

The Ludra raid requires 2,800 Item Level (iLvl) for Normal mode and 3,000 Item Level for Hard mode. Both modes are immediately unlocked upon launch on November 4, 2026.

When does Ludra Awakens release in AION 2?

Ludra Awakens officially releases on November 4, 2026 at 15:00 CET (06:00 PST). The pre-raid Odyle Energy Boost event begins earlier on October 28 after maintenance.

How does the Pre-Event Odyle Energy Boost work?

From October 28 after maintenance until November 11 before maintenance (CET), every character that logs in receives 2x Odyle Energy daily via in-game mail. Up to 28 Odyle Energy total can be collected over 14 days (mail expires in 7 days).

How do you earn the Conqueror of Ludra and purple decorative titles?

The 1st party to clear Hard Mode Ludra in each region earns 'Conqueror of Ludra' with a purple glow. Parties 2–5 earn 'Champion of Ludra', and parties 6–20 earn 'Challenger of Ludra'. All other players clearing Normal or Hard earn 'Ludra Hunter'.

Which dungeons count for Daeva Bootcamp global milestones?

Eligible Conquest dungeons are Krao Cave, Draupnir, Urugugu Canyon, and Vakron Sky Island (Normal/Hard). Eligible Transcendence dungeons are Shattered Arkanis and Deus Research Base (all stages). Reaching 7M clears rewards up to 14 Clear Tickets and 35 Resurrection Stones.

What are the Twitch Drops rewards for Ludra Awakens?

Watching 14 hours of AION 2 streams on Twitch between Nov 4 and Nov 18 unlocks 14 tiers of rewards, including Odyle Energy, Soul Codex, Resurrection Spiritstones, Dotted Glasses (12h), and the Black Conical Hat helm (14h).

Is the Halloween Event and Pumpkin Coin Calculator still active?

Yes! The Halloween event runs until November 4, 2026. You can switch to the Halloween & Abyss tab at any time to access the interactive Pumpkin Coin calculator, Candy Key recipes, and the Free Skeleton Skin guide.

Where can I view previous patch notes and banwave updates?

You can use the patch selector in the top navbar or visit our comprehensive Patch Archive page to view full snapshots of the Oct 21 Security Banwave (15,380 accounts) and the Oct 14 Halloween patch.
