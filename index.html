-6.5% Boss HP

Security, PvP & Game Improvements

Anti-Bot Measures, Lower Abyss PvP & Quality of Life

Anti-Bot Gathering & Account Protections

  • • Minimum Gathering Level: Raised from Lv. 10 ➔ Lv. 45.
  • • Daily Gathering Cap: Maximum 1,000 gathers per day per server (resets daily at 08:00 CET / 00:00 PDT).
  • • Character Deletion Waiting Period: Increased from 1 day ➔ 7 days to prevent bot account recycling.
  • • AFK Lobby Return: Inactivity kick timer reduced from 3 hours ➔ 30 minutes.

World & Lower Abyss PvP Adjustments

  • • Hidden Cubes: Spawn rate and frequency increased across the open world.
  • • Abyss Entry Limit: Player capacity inside the Abyss significantly increased.
  • • Dimensional Core HP & Dissipation of Power: Core HP increased. If an Artifact battle lasts 10 minutes or more, the Dissipation of Power debuff triggers, weakening the Core.

Bug Fixes & System Compensations

  • • Fixed an issue where the revive count occasionally failed to reset upon retrying Expedition Dungeon (Hard).
  • • Removed Supply Requests that mistakenly demanded Sweet Balaur Berry.
  • • Corrected inverted display order of Ariel and Azphel Daevanion in character progression summary.
  • • Fixed an issue where some players became unintentionally invisible during PvP engagements.
  • • Fixed an issue preventing completion of certain Season Missions after defeating the target boss.

Compensations: EU region dungeon entry passes from Oct 25 will be reissued. Unfair duplicate attendance claims from Server Transfer have been reclaimed.

Frequently Asked Questions (AION 2 Patch & Event Hub)

What are the requirements and rules for Urugugu Canyon & Vakron Sky Island Hard Mode?

Both new Hard Mode dungeons require 2,700 Item Level (iLvl), feature a strict 4-minute Boss Frenzy timer, and limit resurrections to a maximum of 3 revives per player. They drop Ariel Fragments, Philosopher's Stones, and Unique skins.

How much Base HP did classes receive in the October 28 update?

At Level 45, Gladiator and Templar received +1,500 Base HP (increasing from 4,702 to 6,202, +31.9%). Assassin, Ranger, Sorcerer, Spiritmaster, Chanter, and Cleric each received +1,000 Base HP (increasing from 4,702 to 5,702, +21.3%).

How does Crafted Gear Amplify and Gear Transfer work now?

Gear Transfer is now unlocked from Tier 3/2 Expedition Gear to Tier 2/1 Crafted Gear (such as Enraged Kromede Necklace into White Dragon Lord Necklace). Crafted Gear Amplify caps have been increased to Lv. 3 (True/Star Dragon) and Lv. 5 (White/Dark Dragon).

What new skins are featured in Daeva Lookbook Vol. 3?

Daeva Lookbook Vol. 3 includes the Vulpine Sunshower (6-piece set, $29.99) and No-Nonsense Maid (4-piece set, $29.99) armor sets, Secret Teatime weapon skins ($11.99 each), Maid Ribbon Wings ($19.99), Azure Flame Kumiho and Glossy Pink Puppy pets ($19.99 each), and exclusive Quna helms & eyewear.

What are the new anti-bot and gathering restrictions?

The minimum gathering level has been raised from Lv. 10 to Lv. 45, and a strict server-wide cap of 1,000 gathers per day has been enforced. Character deletion now requires a 7-day waiting period, and the AFK lobby return timer is shortened to 30 minutes.

What rewards are included in the 28-Day Attendance Event 'Atreia's Call'?

Over the 28 days, players accumulate 8x Odyle Energy, 8,000 Soul Crystals, 40x Battle Enhance Scrolls, 30x Resurrection Spiritstones, 20x Roasted Fogus, 20x Life Crystals, 6x Soul Codex: Reset, 2x Ticket Selection Chests, and 4x Unknown Fissure Tickets.

When does the Ludra Awakens Raid launch?

Ludra Awakens officially launches on November 4, 2026 at 15:00 CET. The pre-event 2x Odyle Energy daily mail boost is already active from October 28 to help all players reach the 2,800 / 3,000 iLvl requirement.

Is the Halloween Event and Pumpkin Coin Shop still active?

Yes, the Halloween event runs until November 4, 2026. You can switch to the Halloween & Abyss tab at any time to access the interactive Pumpkin Coin calculator, Candy Key recipes, and the Free Skeleton Skin guide.
