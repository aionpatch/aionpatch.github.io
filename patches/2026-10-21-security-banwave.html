Temporary Security Lock vs Permanent Ban

Temporary Security Lock

Precautionary • NOT a Penalty

Triggered automatically when NCSoft security algorithms detect a connection or hardware signature drastically differing from your normal gameplay (e.g., VPNs, public WiFi, new PC, or IP jumps). It shields your items and characters from being liquidated if compromised!

How to Restore Access:
  1. Visit the official Support Portal: help.plaync.com.
  2. Submit an inquiry with your registered email and character details.
  3. Once ownership is verified, the security lock is removed immediately.
Submit Support Ticket

Permanent Integrated Ban

Policy Penalty • 15-Day Appeal

Issued for severe violations outlined in the Operation Policy Sanctions Schedule. This terminates game access across all linked game servers and Steam IDs for botting, RMT operations, or client tampering.

Formal Appeal Process:
  • • Appeals must be lodged within 15 days of the restriction.
  • • Each appeal triggers a manual second-look investigation of server logs.
  • • False positives are reversed with item/progress restoration.
File Ban Appeal

Quick Answers & Official Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions (Oct 21 Security Update)

How many accounts were banned in the Oct 21 update?

NCSoft placed permanent integrated account bans on 15,380 accounts found operating bots, macro automation scripts, gold farming networks, or illicit Real-Money Trading (RMT).

Can I be banned for long manual grinding sessions?

No. NCSoft explicitly stated that regular gameplay, including lengthy grinding sessions, is 100% unaffected. Sanctions are only executed after rigorous verification against botting heuristics.

What should I do if my account received a Security Lock?

A Security Lock is a defense mechanism to prevent account theft. Simply visit help.plaync.com/faq/aion2global, submit a ticket verifying your identity, and access will be unlocked.

How much time do I have to appeal a false ban?

Under Article 7 of the PlayNC Operation Policy, you have 15 days from the date of the sanction to submit a formal appeal ticket through Customer Support.
